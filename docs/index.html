How I approach engineering

Explicit boundaries

Keep one source of truth for important state. Define ownership, inputs, error handling, and integration contracts.

Proof over polish

Provide ways to validate behavior. Separate completed capabilities from partial implementations and future targets.

Practical constraints

Favor maintainable code, restrained dependencies, accessible hardware paths, and non-destructive changes.
